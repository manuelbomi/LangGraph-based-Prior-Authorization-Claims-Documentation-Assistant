POLICY TITLE: Medical Necessity Criteria for Lumbar Spine MRI Without Contrast

CRITERION a: Low back pain symptoms have been present for greater than 6 weeks.
CRITERION b: Conservative treatment (for example, physical therapy, home exercise, NSAIDs, or activity modification) has been attempted for at least 6 weeks without adequate improvement.
CRITERION c: A documented neurological deficit (for example, motor weakness, diminished reflexes, or sensory loss in a dermatomal pattern) OR a red-flag symptom (for example, bowel or bladder dysfunction, saddle anesthesia, unexplained weight loss, history of cancer, or fever) is present.
CRITERION d: If a lumbar spine X-ray was already obtained, it did not adequately explain the patient's symptoms, or an X-ray was not performed.
CRITERION e: The requesting provider has documented that MRI findings are expected to change management (for example, surgical referral, injection planning, or ruling out a serious underlying cause).
